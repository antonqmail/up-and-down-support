Privacy Policy

Effective 12 November 2026

Golf Slope is an offline iPhone tool for measuring local slope during putting practice. This policy describes the app and its support website. The developer is Anton Khersonsky. Contact anton@qmail.ae with privacy questions.

On-device processing

Motion readings, slope measurements and aiming estimates are processed on your iPhone. They are not uploaded to us or recorded as a motion history. The current measurement is held temporarily while the app is in use. Motion sensing stops when the app is not active.

Saved preferences

Your last distance and units, step length, green speed, appearance and calibration offset are stored locally in the app’s preferences. We do not receive these values. Reset saved calibration removes the saved level correction. Deleting the app removes its local app data. Apple manages any device backups according to your backup settings; deleting local data does not automatically remove existing backups.

No tracking or accounts

The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics SDKs or developer-operated backend. It does not collect location, contacts, photos, camera images or payment information. We do not sell app data, share it for advertising, or track you across apps and websites.

Support requests

If you email support, we receive the email address, message and attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your request and troubleshoot the app. Our email service providers handle that correspondence. Please do not send sensitive information. We keep correspondence only as long as needed to handle the request and meet applicable legal obligations. You can request access, correction or deletion by emailing the address above.

Website and external links

This support website does not add analytics, advertising cookies or contact forms. Its hosting provider may process technical request information, including IP address and browser information, to deliver and secure the site. Settings includes links to this website and the Up and Down App Store page. When you open these links, Apple or the website provider handles the request under its own privacy policy.

Children

Golf Slope does not create profiles or knowingly collect personal information from children. A parent or guardian can contact us about information voluntarily included in a support request.

Changes

We will update the effective date if this policy changes. For any privacy question, contact anton@qmail.ae.
