A SIMPLE TOOL FOR PUTTING PRACTICE

Read the slope.
Find your line.

Golf Slope turns your iPhone into a simple practice-green level, with clear slope readings and a visual aiming guide.

Email support ↗

For help, email anton@qmail.ae. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version and what happened. Screenshots are optional; remove personal information first.

Take a reading

Place your iPhone screen-up on a firm, stable surface, with its reference arrow pointing toward the hole. After a brief still period, the app holds the measured left-to-right and uphill/downhill slope. Lift and replace the phone for a fresh reading. Motion sensing pauses while the app is in the background.

“Place flat to read or calibrate”

The measuring range is up to 6% in either direction. Place the phone screen-up with consistent support. A camera bump, rocking case or uneven grass can change the reading. Use Quick calibration on a known level surface to correct how your phone and case rest. Do not calibrate on an unknown green slope.

Calibration

Quick calibration shows the current measured surface and the saved zero offset. Keep the phone still on a known level surface, then tap Save level surface. Reset saved calibration removes the correction. Recalibrate after changing your case or the way the phone rests.

Distance and units

The first launch starts at 3 steps. The app remembers the last distance and units you select: steps, feet, yards or meters. Set your normal step length in Settings. Choose ADD for a direction reference without a distance-based aim adjustment. Choose a distance and green speed for an estimated starting line.

Two aiming views

The rotate icon switches the aiming direction between the left edge and the top edge of the wide screen. Align the displayed reference arrow toward the hole after switching. The red guide continues the marker line on the ball; the red bar indicates a square putter face.

Appearance

Choose signature yellow, light chalk or black in Settings. Your choice is saved and applies to the entire app.

What the estimate means

Only the surface under the phone is measured. The aim estimate assumes a uniform slope, no grain, the selected green speed and about 12 inches of finish allowance. It does not scan the entire green or guarantee a result. This is a practice tool; electronic slope measurement and alignment aids are restricted during rounds played under the Rules of Golf.

Privacy

No account, advertising or analytics. Motion readings and calculations stay on your iPhone. Read the privacy policy.
